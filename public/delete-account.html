FitBite Pro

Delete account and data

FitBite Pro stores your profile, meals, workouts, and chat on this phone. Deleting the account erases that local data. Replace the email below before you publish this page.

In the app

  1. Open FitBite Pro.
  2. Go to the Me tab.
  3. Tap Delete Account & Erase All Local Data.
  4. Read the confirmation, then tap Delete everything.

The app reloads with an empty profile. This cannot be undone.

By email

If you cannot open the app, email hello@yourdomain.com from the address you used in the app and ask for your local data to be deleted. There is no separate cloud account in this build, so uninstalling the app also removes the on-device copy.
