FitBite Pro

Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 November 2026.

What stays on the phone

Profile, meals, water, workouts, chat, daily quotas, and settings are stored on the device. FitBite Pro does not run its own account server. Signing out or deleting the account erases that local data.

Camera, microphone, location, and Bluetooth

The app asks for a permission only after you tap the related control. The camera is used for a food photo you choose to analyze. The microphone is used for voice-to-text you start. Location is used for a workout route you start. Bluetooth is used when you pair a watch. These are not collected at launch.

AI providers

When you send a coach message, kitchen request, food photo, or daily challenge, that content is sent to the configured AI provider so it can answer. Audio is sent only when you start transcription. Nothing is sent just because the app is open.

Wellness, not medicine

For general wellness and fitness tracking only — not a medical device. AI nutrition and Halal indicators are estimates; always verify product labels.

Contact

Privacy questions and deletion requests are handled in the app from Me, then Privacy & Data.
